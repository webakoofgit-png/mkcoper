Campus Life

  • Academic Rigor

    Our pharmacy college's campus is characterized by intense academic endeavors.

  • Laboratory Experience

    Students carry out experiments, examine data, and acquire real-world experience that will be crucial to their future employment in pharmacy.

  • Student Organizations

    Campus life is enriched by various student organizations dedicated to pharmacy related interests.

  • Cultural Events

    Annual cultural festivals featuring music, dance, drama, and art competitions.

  • Health Camps and Awareness Drives

    Organization of free health camps in nearby communities to provide basic medical check-ups and health education.
