Training & Placement Cell

The Training & Placement Cell works as a bridge between students and the pharmaceutical industry. It supports career readiness through structured training, industry interaction, internships and placement facilitation. The framework below is aligned with common Training & Placement practices followed in pharmacy institutions.

Vision

To develop competent, confident and industry-ready pharmacy graduates by integrating academic excellence, practical exposure and professional ethics in every stage of student development.

Mission & Objectives

  • Strengthen institute-industry interaction through MoUs, guest lectures and industrial visits.
  • Provide continuous placement preparation in aptitude, communication, resume writing, GD and interviews.
  • Facilitate internships and project exposure to improve real-world pharmaceutical skills.
  • Support students for diverse career pathways in production, QA/QC, regulatory and healthcare sectors.
  • Promote ethical, inclusive and transparent placement opportunities for all eligible students.

Committees

Unit Composition Role
Training & Placement Cell TPO / Coordinator, Departmental Coordinators, Faculty Mentors, Student Coordinators Planning and execution of placement, internship and training activities
Industry Interface Team Faculty representatives and external industry mentors Recruiter connect, pre-placement talks, collaboration and skill-gap feedback
Student Placement Team Final year representatives from D.Pharm and B.Pharm Drive communication, participation tracking and placement support coordination

Placement Record

  • Campus and off-campus recruitment support for final-year students.
  • Opportunities mapped across Production, QA/QC, Regulatory Affairs, Sales & Marketing and Community Pharmacy.
  • Regular engagement with pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, retail chains and allied healthcare organizations.
Latest batch-wise placement statistics are published by the placement cell after annual verification.

Training Programs

Aptitude and reasoning practice modules
Communication and personality development sessions
Resume writing and professional email drafting
Mock interviews and group discussion drills
Pharmacy domain orientation (QA/QC, RA, PV, Production)
Industrial visits, expert talks and pre-placement interactions
