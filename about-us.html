About Us

About MKCOPER

Mahadev Kanchan College of Pharmaceutical Education and Research is committed to building skilled, ethical and research-oriented pharmacy professionals. We focus on quality academics, modern learning resources and industry-relevant training to support student growth and community healthcare outcomes.

Our institution combines strong classroom teaching with practical laboratory exposure, mentorship and value-based education. We prepare students for careers in healthcare, industry and research with confidence, discipline and social responsibility.

With experienced faculty, modern laboratories and a supportive academic environment, MKCOPER encourages innovation, critical thinking and professional ethics in every stage of learning.

Through seminars, industrial interaction, community outreach and skill-development activities, we help students become competent pharmacists who can contribute effectively to society and the healthcare sector.

Vision

  • Develop an excellent pharmacy college through research in current trends in pharmaceutical sciences and technology.
  • Create knowledgeable pharmacy professionals for healthcare and society.
  • Strengthen pharmacy education, research and patient care to improve health and wellness outcomes.

Mission

  • Deliver academic excellence through quality infrastructure and recent technologies in pharmaceutical sciences.
  • Prepare students through student-centric teaching and learning for industry, research and social needs.
  • Serve society through the appropriate use of medications, devices and pharmaceutical services.
  • Build self-motivated, independent and socially responsible pharmacists.

Approvals

  • Approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
  • Approved by Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), New Delhi.
  • Affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere.
  • Affiliated to Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai.
  • PCI Sanction Intake Letter 2025-26
  • MSBTE Sanction Intake Letter 2025-26
  • DBATU Affiliation Letter 2025-26
  • Recognized under applicable norms of the competent state technical education authorities.
