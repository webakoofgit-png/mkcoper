Hostel Facilities
At Mahadev Kanchan College of Pharmaceutical Education & Research

Mahadev Kanchan College of Pharmaceutical Education & Research is committed to providing a comfortable and secure living environment for its students. Our hostel facilities are designed to offer a home-away-from-home experience, ensuring that students can focus on their studies and personal growth without any concerns about their living conditions. Here are the major hostel facilities we provide:

1.Accommodation:
1.Separate hostels for male and female students.
2.Well-furnished rooms with options for single, double, and triple occupancy.
3.Study tables, chairs, beds, and wardrobes provided in each room.
2.Safety and Security:
1.24/7 security with CCTV surveillance to ensure the safety of all residents.
2.Resident wardens and security personnel available round-the-clock.
3.Dining Facilities:
1.Hygienic and nutritious meals served in the hostel mess.
2.Balanced diet with a variety of vegetarian and non-vegetarian options.
3.Special care taken for dietary restrictions and preferences of students.
3.Recreational Facilities:
1.Common rooms equipped with television, indoor games, and seating arrangements.
2.Sports facilities including a playground for outdoor games like volleyball, basketball, and badminton.
3.Organized cultural and recreational activities to foster a vibrant community life.
4.Internet and Connectivity:
1.High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity throughout the hostel premises.
2.Dedicated study areas with internet access to support academic work.
5.Healthcare and Well-being:
1.On-call medical services and a tie-up with nearby hospitals for emergencies. 2.Regular health check-ups and wellness programs organized for students.

6.Housekeeping and Maintenance:
1.Regular housekeeping services to maintain cleanliness and hygiene in the hostels.
2.Prompt maintenance services for any repair or maintenance issues.
7.Laundry Services:
In-house laundry facilities available for students to ensure clean and fresh clothing.
8.Transport:
1.Shuttle services for easy transportation to and from the college campus.
2.Regular bus services for students to access nearby facilities and the city center.
9.Additional Facilities:
1.Access to a reading room and library for quiet study sessions.
2.Availability of vending machines for snacks and beverages.
